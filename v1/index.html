About Me

Hello, I’m James McKenna. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, with a concentration in Software, Systems, and Networks.

I have worked on various personal software development projects involving databases and data analytics. These projects focused on pulling, structuring, and analyzing real-world data, particularly within the cryptocurrency space, where I have found it easy to test ideas and build practical tools.

During my senior year, I worked in UNC Charlotte’s IT Enterprise Infrastructure department as an Associate Network Technician under Ozlem Cankurtaran. In that role, I gained hands-on experience with enterprise network infrastructure, troubleshooting, ticketing systems, equipment coordination, and collaboration across multiple departments.

I was also heavily involved with the UNC Charlotte Men’s Volleyball Club, serving as Social Media Manager, Vice President, President, and now Graduated Advisor. I managed budgets up to $10,000, coordinated equipment orders, led a team of eight officers, and helped organize a 175+ attendee arena event.
