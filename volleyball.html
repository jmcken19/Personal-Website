Volleyball

Clips from my senior season at UNC Charlotte as team captain

Impact

Coordinated the first Men's Volleyball Club Match in the Halton Arena

This had been a dream of mine since my freshman year. In the summer of 2025, while working a ticket to pick up a box of old phones from the building manager’s office in Halton Arena, I saw an opportunity to bring the idea up. That conversation opened my eyes to how difficult the process would be. There were scheduling conflicts with the school’s NCAA basketball and volleyball teams, budgeting concerns, Halton Arena compliance requirements, and several other hurdles. For months, I kept following up. After three months with no real progress, I finally received a list of possible dates in December. Then came two more months of silence. On February 3rd, I was finally told the event was approved for February 12th. That gave me only nine days to plan everything. Even with the short timeline, the event ended up being one of the most meaningful moments of my life and time with the club.

Landed a vendor deal for new jerseys

My leadership progression within the club spans from Social Media Manager (2022–2023) to Vice President and President (2023–2024), Advisor (2024–2025), and returning as President for the 2025–2026 academic year. As President and team captain, I serve as the primary representative of the club, leading executive board meetings, aligning officer goals, and overseeing the overall vision and direction of the organization to maintain our status as a competitive Division I club team.

Club Operations

Running a collegiate club sport requires rigorous operational management. I oversee budgeting, campus recreation compliance, scheduling, and travel logistics. To ensure the club's longevity, I establish clear channels of communication, delegate key tasks to officers, and manage smooth transitions by documenting standard operating procedures, ensuring incoming officers are prepared to manage the club's administration and resources.

Collaboration Events

I have directed several highly successful events on campus, coordinating all facets of tournament execution. This includes organizing the UNC Charlotte Social Volleyball Tournaments and the Grass Volleyball Tournament, with turnouts of up to 70+ participants. Responsibilities involved facility booking, equipment setup, designing competitive tournament brackets, securing sponsorships, and managing on-site operations. Additionally, I managed the logistics and compliance for our center-court Halton Arena Match, drawing over 200 spectators.

Member Development

Through my efforts, the UNC Charlotte Men’s Volleyball Club has achieved record event turnouts, increased student engagement, and stronger financial stability. By designing structured transition guides and fostering collaborative relationships with campus partners and sponsors, I have established a professional foundation that enables future officers to lead effectively and ensures the club remains a premier athletic organization at UNC Charlotte.
