About me

I graduated from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science, concentration in Software, Systems, and Networks. I'm currently working at Duke Energy on the Telecom Lifecycle Team.

Prior to Duke Energy, I worked in UNC Charlotte's IT Enterprise Infrastructure department as an Associate Network Technician, where I gained hands-on experience with enterprise network hardware, compliance audits, and cross-team coordination.

Outside of work I build projects using AI tools and have been testing projects with blockchain data. I also serve as a graduated advisor for the UNC Charlotte Men's Volleyball Club, where I spent four years working my way up from Social Media Manager to Vice President, President, and Team Captain.

After graduation I spent a month backpacking through Europe. Switzerland was a highlight, with favorites including Zermatt, Grindelwald, Bern, and Interlaken. From there I headed to Munich, through Austria, and into Italy, stopping in Milan, Lido di Spina, and Florence. China and Japan are on the list next, though I take up any travel opportunity that comes my way.
