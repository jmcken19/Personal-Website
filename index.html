About me

I graduated from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science, concentration in Software, Systems, and Networks. I currently work at Duke Energy as a Junior Network Analyst on the Telecom Lifecycle Team, supporting the deployment, configuration, troubleshooting, and lifecycle management of network devices across fiber, microwave, and RTU communications networks in a NERC CIP-regulated environment.

Before Duke Energy, I worked in UNC Charlotte's IT Enterprise Infrastructure department as an Associate Network Technician during my senior year, where I got experience with enterprise networking infrastructure, device management, and troubleshooting across campus hardware and software devices.

I founded NextOne after recognizing a gap in professional development resources for club sport athletes during my time with the Men's Volleyball Club. NextOne provides direct and personable mentorship, professional development, and career guidance to collegiate club athletes across multiple sports and universities. I am very committed to helping the next generation of club athletes find their path — Cohort 1 is currently in session.

Outside of work, I serve as a graduated advisor for the UNC Charlotte Men's Volleyball Club. Over four years as an active member, I worked my way up from Social Media Manager to Vice President, President, and Team Captain. Now I help the guys run the program from an outside perspective, bringing experience and continuity to each new group of leaders and ensuring smooth transitions and management.

After graduation, I spent a month backpacking through Europe, visiting Switzerland, Germany, Austria, and Italy. I also studied abroad in the Netherlands earlier in my college career.
